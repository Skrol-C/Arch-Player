# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

android

## Users

The developer themself, on their own phone, listening to their own local music.
No shared audience, no accounts, no onboarding funnel to serve.

## Product Purpose

A personal Android music player for local files that feels good to play music on:
very visual, premium-feeling, and eventually a one-stop shop with in-app music
downloading (Needle). Success means opening it daily instead of any other player.

## Positioning

The most visual-feeling local player: real refractive liquid-glass motion plus
downloading inside the app so music never has to come from somewhere else.
SpotiFLAC's glass pill is the visual bar to meet or beat.

## Operating Context

Sideloaded debug APK installed over Wi-Fi. Source material is the user's own
MP3s in self-picked folders. No streaming service, no server, no sync.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Confirmed: Flutter + liquid_glass_easy refractive lenses (Impeller),
  shader warmup in main() so first frame is glass, not frost.
- To rebuild: local scan, folder toggles, Media3-equivalent playback via
  just_audio + audio_service, Songs/Albums/Artists, simple player.
- Explicitly undecided: Needle (in-app downloading — name locked, no source,
  format, or storage design agreed yet).

## Brand Commitments

Arch red (#FA1744) on near-black, red reserved for accents. Apple Music-grade
premium look. Library first. User-volunteered, binding.

## Evidence on Hand

Glass-pill trial build on the user's phone (DNY-NX9, Android 16). Prior
native builds proved scan + playback + folder sheet; to be re-proven in
Flutter. No testimonials, press, or bundled assets.

## Product Principles

1. Feel first: every screen moves; a static screen is a bug.
2. Library before theater: browsing music must be premium before anything
   theatrical is added.
3. One-stop shop trajectory: every addition should serve playing or getting
   music, never clutter.
4. Personal scale: no growth features, no accounts, no analytics surface.
